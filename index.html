    App Guide

    Getting started

    Setlist Builder is a shared, searchable song index for browsing worship songs and preparing personal setlists. The published index is not a centrally synchronized service schedule.

    Role Views

    Tap the role indicator in the header to choose Planner, Singer, Keys, Guitar, or Drums. Each role prioritizes useful information without changing the underlying song data.

    Browsing & appearance

    Choose Compact for quick browsing or Detailed for more information. Tap a song card or its expand button to open details.

    The right drawer holds sorting, view settings, and Appearance: the app theme (Paper, Sage, Midnight or Royal) and S/M/L text sizes. The header hides as you scroll down and returns as you scroll up; the bottom search bar stays within reach.

    Open the filter drawer from the header filter button. Tap keys, BPM values, or tags on cards or in the drawer to filter; BPM matches within ±3. Clear active filters to broaden results.

    Personal setlists

    Open Setlist Builder and choose Sunday Service or Worship Night. Long-press a song to add it to either list; Planner also offers add buttons in expanded cards. Use the star to save Favorites, then add them from the Favorites tab.

    In your setlist, use ▲/▼ to reorder and ✕ to remove songs. Choose a performance key, including Custom Key. Tap 📝 to edit weekly notes and an optional weekly YouTube link.

    Share offers text or Screenshot Mode with three themes. Select the details to include; weekly notes require explicit selection each time. YouTube URLs are text-only. Copy Text opens selectable text: long-press to copy on mobile.

    Stored on this browser: personal setlists, weekly notes, Favorites, and preferences do not automatically synchronize with other devices or worship-team members. Clearing browser data may remove them.

    Gestures: swipe inward from the left edge for filters or the right edge for settings; swipe back toward that edge to close. Swipe up from the search bar for Setlist Builder. Long-press also offers Favorites and Copy Song Info shortcuts.

    External resources

    YouTube links open recordings; a browse card may offer a YouTube search when no recording is saved. CCLI/SongSelect links open licensed song resources. Missing or unavailable external links may not appear. Sharing uses saved links only.

    The app does not host chord charts, copyrighted lyrics, or recordings.

    USER / ADMIN

    USER is the default presentation. ADMIN adds local song editing, export, and raw-record inspection. It is not a login or permission system, and local edits do not update the published website for other users.

    Indicators & history

    🎙️ Bright: in your active setlist. Faded: in another personal setlist. A filled star marks a Favorite.

    Trending means 3+ recorded plays in the last six months. Current Role Views show play counts and history dates instead of the older flame badge.

    >2y marks songs with no recorded history in this index (legacy/untracked). The “Hide Legacy Songs” setting hides those entries; it does not calculate a rolling two-year cutoff.
